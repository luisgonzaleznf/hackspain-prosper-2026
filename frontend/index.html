What happens on every call.

The voice is the part you hear. These six steps are the part that matters.

Identify

A name and two identifiers before anything is written.

Look up

The chart, the note and the visits, read before it offers anything.

Offer

Only real slots, and only ones the plan covers.

Confirm

Site, doctor, day and hour, read back in the caller's language.

Write

One action per request. Or a refusal, with the reason.

Explain

Every sentence opens to show the lookups and the rule behind it.
